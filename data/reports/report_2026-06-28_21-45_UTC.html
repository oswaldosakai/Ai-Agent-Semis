AMD's MI300X and MI400 ramp, as well as Broadcom's custom ASIC programs for hyperscalers, face analogous single-source exposure. For equipment vendors, the read is more nuanced. AMAT, Lam Research, and ASML derive revenue when TSMC places tool orders and accepts delivery — any capacity expansion delay compresses near-term equipment revenue recognition, though backlog visibility at ASML (with its €40+ billion order book) provides a buffer. Investors should watch TSMC's July earnings call (expected mid-July) for any revision to 2026 capacity guidance or commentary on fab staffing ratios and water reclamation progress.
